## Discussion

**On your poster,** say what your results mean and where their limits are.

**Arrange the boxes** in *poster.yaml*. Its layout map is a small picture of the poster: every word is a box and shows the file of the same name in *sections*.

```text
layout: |
  background  methods
  results     results
  discussion  conclusion
```

A name written twice makes its box twice as wide or as tall. **+** next to *Content Boxes* on the left adds a box.
